# Guard native edits to an owned composer range

## Why
The accepted key/snapshot source preserves exact event and text facts but cannot authorize Submit, rendering or clearing. A usable composer needs native range ownership and a live compare at the writer boundary. Historical offsets, visible headers, a cached revision or ordinary undo cannot provide that authority.

## What Changes
- Establish a private exact-build plain-text writer profile: existing default layout, bounded whole-document shape, closed native/IME/Qt edit scopes and quiet undo state.
- Provision and reacquire two native LF anchors through verified native insertion/readback and the existing document/page binding, with bounded live ID/position correspondence and explicit namespace limits.
- Compare exact current owner, anchor range, text and revision after all preflight getters before a private native action, with no synchronous consumer callback or event pumping across apply/settlement.
- Return copied refusal/attempted/unknown/settled facts while preserving all text outside the exclusive anchored range. Do not infer a rollback or clear from a historical read.
- Keep product Submit grammar and existing Buddy dispatcher/ledger/render ownership in Buddy. Coordinate only an implementable copied request/result contract; no second conversation store or fabricated currentness.

## Impact
SDK native composer source, private exact-build profile and owned fixtures. Paired consumer specification and Buddy integration remain Root-owned until exact files/contracts are coordinated. This change is unenabled until separately qualified; no device action, activation, provider call or release admission follows from source work.
