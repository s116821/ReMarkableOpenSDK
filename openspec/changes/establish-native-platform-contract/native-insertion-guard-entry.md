## Result, cancellation and retained ownership

A positive diagnostic witness requires the uncontested terminal Core mismatch,
private catch, one outer/inner entry, zero tracked insertion forwarding, a
nonzero full-width attributed job, one exact same-worker completion and one
ordinary operation callback, with no corresponding pageAdded. Keep these facts
separate in the closed result schema. Greater-ID completion, normal callback
alone and absence of an observed signal alone cannot establish success.

Use the original absolute request/recovery budgets, not renewed relative
windows. Cancellation/deadline/receiver loss revokes normal-result permission
and preserves unknown outcomes without resubmission. Strong native owners and
pending callback state remain in intentionally resident heap storage through
candidate exit; this experiment does not test last-owner reclamation.

The independent actor restores stock even if the modified UI, Entry or parent
controller fails. Read back the complete disposable fixture and every original
file/page object, ID/order/PDF mapping/ink/unknown field, and verify provider/
service policy and evidence archives before removing only owned inactive RAM
files. A native mismatch witness is not accepted creation, product integration,
safe shutdown/unload, general cancellation or supported capability.
