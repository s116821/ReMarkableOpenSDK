# One intended-target writing and reopen sequence

Use the existing twelve-page fixture and intended target UUID; do not create
another page. A new default-off research Entry will compose already reviewed
mechanisms under one original setup/operation deadline and independent actor.
It must not change existing Entry modes or advertise a supported capability.

1. Bind one app/window/root/engine and private request directory, original process
   identity, one request claim and absolute phase deadline. Require the separate
   [pen holder](fixed-pen-holder.md) ready and independently recoverable.
2. Dispatch one existing-fixture stock route, then obtain fresh expected document,
   page ID/index/count and ready creation join. Capture the intended blank target
   with the synchronous identity-bracketed helper into a fresh private subfolder.
3. After capture, obtain a separate fresh callback-local ready join. Atomically
   publish the sole pen-go.request as its terminal action. Copy scalar facts only;
   no borrowed receiver or guard escapes. Retain possible effect before dispatch.
4. Await the holder's strictly bound original-byte result under the original end.
   Require child gone, complete fixed dispatch/contact/endpoints, positive
   observed release, closed descriptors and complete trace. Unknown/refusal stops
   progression without redraw. The recovery holder remains independent of UI
   cancellation or process/service restoration.
5. Take a fresh same-target identity-bracketed capture in a second private folder.
   Then run the one-use [ordinary close helper](ordinary-reader-close.md) from a
   fresh ready source join. Dispatched close is reconciliation-required.
6. On a later queued GUI opportunity, observe closure through the same known
   MainView/DocumentView loader composition with no selected document. A missing
   arbitrary root is not closure evidence. Do not hold the old source guard.
7. Dispatch the existing-fixture route once to reopen, then take a separate fresh
   ready identity/full-order join and capture into a third private folder.
   Independently compare target ink and full before/after document backups.

Every capture has its own consumed claim and inode-pinned image/scalar pair, with
no overwrite. Existing caps remain per image/pair; a new closed combined scalar
must bind original bytes/hashes and phase/clock/retirement facts. Retire each
phase's request-owned objects/descriptors before a later queued phase while
keeping native/application owners resident. No runtime pointer address becomes
an external identity or lifetime lease.

Use real owned QObject/QML/files/queued lifetime controls with explicit modeled
native joins and pen IO. Inject stale/duplicate/lost phase delivery, final-callout
deletion/permission/metadata changes, child loss and overruns. A whole composed
success needs separately frozen source/artifact/provider/current-state/fixture,
original-byte consumers and independent pen/UI recovery. No native action follows
from this plan. UI closure and stable file reads/reopen are sampled persistence
evidence, not a crash-durable storage fence or universal writer exclusion.

The closure observer may reuse the legacy composition census through a separate
read-only callback. This path must not select or invoke the navigator's open
method. Its borrowed main/context/guard stay callback-local and the composition
is rechecked by the callback's final guard while its own watchers remain live;
no provider getter or permit callback follows that terminal snapshot. A fresh MainView lexical context must bind its
root and DocumentView Loader; the Loader must be synchronous, Ready, hidden,
with a nonnull item whose typed document pointer is null and documentLoaded is
false. MainView documentViewActive must be false. Subscribe before getters and
refuse lifetime, notified ABA, metadata, context, permission or deadline changes.
An unloaded, missing, busy, unrelated or merely invisible Reader is insufficient.
