## Integration meaning and remaining questions

The mechanism now has one composed native example: conditional creation after
the fixed original source, matched completion and exact post-order, one verified
adjacent turn when the Reader remains on source, then a fresh ready-target join.
Consumers must preserve these distinct facts. Remembered metadata, dispatch
acceptance and callbacks individually do not establish current-page identity.
After an ambiguous committed result, reconcile without repeating the mutation.

This is sampled idle-fixture admission, not atomic exclusion of all input and
writers. Stock Next can create at document end and reread currentPage around
text-mode callouts; the verified post-insertion order excludes the observed end
branch for this trial. Readiness does not establish rendered blank pixels.

Useful identity-bracketed capture, blank-content corroboration, write/navigation/
reopen, notebook/end coverage, resident mutation/failure handling and owner
reclamation remain open. Logical request retirement is distinct from unloading
the resident adapter. Restoration does not repair or explain the historical
rendering shutdown crash. Other firmware/models need separate qualification.
The [integration plan](../research/native-adapter-integration-plan.md), supported
capability and broader OpenSpec change remain unfinished.
