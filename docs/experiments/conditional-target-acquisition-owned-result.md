## Implemented research behavior

`wantTargetAcquisition` defaults false and requires conditional forwarding. Before
native owner acquisition, the coordinator admits one public Method/Slot
`moveForward()` with no parameters and void/QVariant return, recording bounded
owned metadata. Unsupported metadata refuses before probe, registration or
creation. Reader and document QPointer identities remain pinned across subsequent
joins in the original process/window/engine and deadline.

After the matched normal forward, callback, completion and indexed pageAdded,
the existing public-metaobject observer checks the complete expected N+1 order.
The helper rechecks method metadata after guard callouts and consumes a shared
claim before one DirectConnection invocation. Failed or throwing invocation
consumes the claim and reports unknown. An already-selected target skips Next.
The old source Sample is not inspected after intentional dispatch.

A separate observation follows a two-second queued settling opportunity within
the original absolute thirty-second plan. It must rejoin the retained identities,
check the full order again and find the ready target at source+1. The settling
opportunity is an asynchronous wait, not a continuous GUI-thread pause. The Core
and indexed events are rechecked at completion. Post-commit failure retains the
forward fact and requires reconciliation without retrying creation or navigation.

`READER_INSERTION_SELECT_TARGET=1` requires the conditional-forward selector.
Only that mode emits closed schema3; schema1 and schema2 remain separate. The
strict consumer checks original raw bytes, then reuses the unchanged schema2
insertion checks through a local derived validation object. Returned evidence
retains the original schema3 object. Final order is checked in full internally;
its ID array is not duplicated in the target-acquisition wire extension.
