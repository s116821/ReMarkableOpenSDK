## Successful collection protocol

The 9688 operator first obtains a full pinned process/actor/root/window/control
binding. One subsequent remote command performs full admission before publishing
one request and recording its original BOOTTIME lower bound. It uses lightweight
pinned process/control/boot/clock/cancellation checks while awaiting the final
scalar, dispatches the original-byte CLI at most once, then completes full pinned
admission and original-clock checks before exit 0. A negative result, incomplete
frame, timeout, changed owner or failed final check cannot become positive.

The host requires an unambiguous successful transport, fresh final USB route,
and its conservative 30 s budget before and after decoding. The original native
90 s setup/30 s operation/20 s helper and separate 10 s CLI caps remain unchanged.
The host 30 s includes prepublication work, so slow operations can still refuse.
No deadline is renewed and no partial-output retry occurs.

The actual fused transport completed in 22.735 s with exit 0, no timeout,
truncation, transport error or unknown status. Final route observation took 1.953 s
and passed. The original 637,742-byte output splits into a 92-byte publisher prefix
and a 637,650-byte frame containing the unchanged 1,906-byte scalar and635,604-byte
PNG. The fixed frame/byte consumer validates identity, schema, sizes, SHA-256,
PNG structure and complete 1404x1872 decode.

The pure byte consumer still says `external_admission=unverified`; it cannot
establish live file/process facts from copied bytes. The operator's separately
verified full live brackets passed. These are complementary evidence layers.
