## Coordinator behavior

`wantConditionalForward` defaults false. Its explicit form uses the correct
source, forbids the wrong-source index and post-rejection probe, freezes a
separate condition and supplies qualified caller assertions. Middle, last and
single-page requests are valid. The old negative path remains separate.

After the ordinary creation call, completion requires a terminal Core normal
forward/return conjunction, one matching indexed `pageAdded`, one matching job
completion and one ordinary callback. A fresh join must retain the same process,
window, engine and GUI Document. The post-order helper reads every N+1 ID and
inverse index, count before/after and typed Document identity before/after. It
requires the original order with exactly the requested target after the source.
The current Reader may still show the source or may show the target; those are
reported separately. Remembered Document page fields do not prove selection.

Only a no-action `worker-source-unready` join or an explicitly unready action
with clean error flags can trigger a bounded read-only rejoin. The fixed join's
unready result also covers its source-error gates; it cannot identify their
subtype. Other lookup/metadata/epoch refusals stop. Retries retain the original
deadline and never repeat creation, registration or the native Task.

The coordinator refreshes the atomic-safe Core snapshot when refusing and before
final publication. A conservative in-flight commit can later settle normally;
it must not permanently latch provisional unknown facts. A committed incomplete
outcome remains unknown and requires reconciliation. Normal forward facts remain
visible after late cancellation even when the success witness is revoked.

uses GUI-thread and QPointer checks, rejects reentry, and brackets every getter,
