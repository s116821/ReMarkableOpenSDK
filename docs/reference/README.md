copy its definitions or claim its unchecked requirements are complete.

Persistent page identity, a current guarded observation and historical evidence
serve different purposes. Restart/replacement invalidates current authority; a
saved receipt or pixel digest cannot recreate it. The linked contracts and
checkpoints are the source basis for these distinctions. Native defaults and
unqualified model/firmware combinations remain subject to their explicit refusal
and qualification gates; consult the current tasks before making support claims.
